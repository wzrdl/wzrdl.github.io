I am a **Research Assistant in [Prof. Feng Liu's Lab](https://rutgers-ninc-lab.github.io/FengLiu/) at Rutgers University**, working at the intersection of **machine learning and brain science**. I received my M.S. in Applied Artificial Intelligence from **Stevens Institute of Technology**.

My current research focuses on **EEG source imaging**, **epilepsy**, and **computational neuroscience**. I develop methods that combine generative models, knowledge graphs, and reinforcement learning to reconstruct brain activity and study seizure localization and propagation. I am particularly interested in how learned priors can complement electrophysiological measurements while respecting the structure of the underlying inverse problem.

My latest work, [*Observable and Null-Space Electrophysiological Source Imaging with Diffusion Priors*](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/415632656_Observable_and_Null-Space_Electrophysiological_Source_Imaging_with_Diffusion_Priors) (**under review**), combines diffusion-based refinement of EEG-observable source activity with deterministic null-space completion. My earlier work includes [knowledge graph representations of seizure semiology and epileptogenic zones](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-32920-z), published in *Scientific Reports*, and *Uncovering epileptic seizure propagation using knowledge graph-based reinforcement learning*, currently under review at *IISE Transactions*.

Previously, I worked with [**Prof. Hao Wang**](https://www.stevens.edu/profile/hwang9) at Stevens on LLM forgetting, continual learning, and federated learning, and with [**Prof. Wensheng Gan**](https://wsgan001.github.io/) at Pazhou Lab, Jinan University, on *Automatic Prompt Optimization for Medicine*. I also coauthored [*COVID-19 infection prediction using physical signs*](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2640976) and interned at **Siemens** on data processing and analysis.

I am actively looking for Ph.D. opportunities and research collaborations in machine learning for neuroscience, neuroimaging, and medical AI.
